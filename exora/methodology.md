# EXORA strategy methodology — working template
1. Document the client's actual offer, service area, evidence, constraints and audience.
2. Identify the buyer problem, differentiator and objections. Do not invent market research.
3. Define one measurable objective, a funnel, channel roles and INR budget.
4. Create content pillars, offers and hypotheses with clear evidence requirements.
5. Require client strategy sign-off before execution; learn from measured outcomes.
This starter must be calibrated with EXORA's marketer on a real client walkthrough, as required by the build plan.
